## 8. Landing Page Plan

### Goal
Turn Instagram visitors and Google searchers into WhatsApp enquiries (vehicle painting) and academy enquiries.

### Structure
1. Hero: looping work video, tagline, WhatsApp button
2. Services: airbrush, freehand, hydro dipping, bike modification, helmets
3. Gallery: best work, before/after
4. How it works: send photo, get quote, drop off, pick up
5. Academy: course info and enquiry button
6. Social proof: 84K followers, embedded reels, YouTube link
7. Location: map, hours, contact form
8. Footer: WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube

### Style (proposal, to be replaced by real branding)
Dark, bold, neon airbrush colour accents; mobile-first (most traffic comes from Instagram on phones).

### Key technical points
- Mobile-first, fast loading, compressed video
- Floating WhatsApp button (wa.me/919677184462)
- SEO: title and meta for Chennai airbrush / hydro dipping / bike modification; LocalBusiness schema (address now known; add hours when received)
- Language: English first; Tamil optional
