- Riders wanting a one-off look (airbrushed tank, hydro-dipped parts). [A]
- Students and hobbyists wanting to learn painting and hydro dipping. [V, the academy exists]

## 7. Positioning and differentiators (evidence-based)
1. **Audience size.** 84.4K Instagram plus 48.1K YouTube is large for a single-studio vehicle customiser. Among the Chennai competitors found (see 05), none showed comparable reach in the sources I could read.
2. **Founder-led brand.** Chandu appears personally in videos, builds recognition and trust, and is a hands-on artist.
3. **Full process transparency.** "Full process" and "full restoration cost" videos show work and cost reasoning.
4. **Airbrush + hydro dip + builds + academy in one studio.** Competitors found are mostly paint/wrap only.
5. **Chennai/Tamil identity.** Titles repeat "Chennai Tamilnadu India" and the phone number.
