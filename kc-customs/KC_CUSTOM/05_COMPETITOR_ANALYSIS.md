## Academy competitors
No Chennai **vehicle airbrush/hydro dip training academy** surfaced. Airbrush course results were makeup academies (e.g. Flowers Beauty Academy, Athenian Academy), which are not comparable. **The KC Customs academy therefore appears unusual locally.** Confirm.

## Patterns across competitors
- Mostly **phone-call CTAs**, little WhatsApp-first or online booking.
- **Prices rarely shown** (only trade listings).
- Portfolios are **small** (3-photo galleries, blog posts), with few before/after.
- Few have **video** on their sites. Several have YouTube videos.
- Specialised or generic. None combine **art + builds + academy + creator reach**.

## Opportunities for KC Customs (no copying)
1. **Lead with video.** KC has 267 YouTube videos and a large Instagram. Competitors' sites are photo-light.
2. **WhatsApp-first enquiry** (send a photo of your bike) instead of "call us".
3. **Own the Yamaha RX niche.** "RX100 / RXZ restoration and custom Chennai" is a specific, content-backed search angle.
4. **Academy as a distinct section.** Likely no direct local rival found.
5. **Real process/transparency.** "Full process" and "restoration cost" videos exist. A "how it works" section with a price-guide policy (once the owner decides) would differentiate.
6. **Founder story.** Chandu's personal brand is a trust asset.
7. **Local SEO** around Chromepet/Tambaram, which none of the above target.
