## 7. How to complete this phase (needs one of these)
1. The owner exports the Instagram data (Settings → Accounts Center → Download your information), or sends the original reel files and captions. **Best option**: it also solves licensing and quality.
2. The user opens the profile logged in and pastes 10–15 reel URLs and captions.
3. Run a logged-in browser session (Claude in Chrome) on the user's machine, with the user's consent, to read reel URLs, captions and view counts.
