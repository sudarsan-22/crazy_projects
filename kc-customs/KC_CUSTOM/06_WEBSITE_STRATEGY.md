# 06. Website Strategy (single landing page)

Based on verified research in 01–05. Nothing here has been built.

## 1. Goals
1. Turn Instagram/YouTube visitors and Google searchers into **WhatsApp enquiries** (+91 96771 84462).
2. Generate **academy enquiries**.
3. Give KC Customs a searchable web presence (it currently has none).

## 2. Primary audiences
- Bike owners wanting custom paint / hydro dip / build / restoration (core: Yamaha RX series, modern Yamaha).
- People who want to learn the craft (academy).

## 4. Key messages (from the brand's own language)
- "Custom airbrush, freehand airbrush & hydro dip studio" (bio)
- "RXZ is an emotion" / #Kcstyle (brand slogan-like usage)
- "Studio + training academy"
- Chennai · Tambaram

## 5. Primary CTA
WhatsApp prefilled message, e.g. "Hi KC Customs, I want to enquire about [service] for my [bike model]." Secondary: call, Instagram, YouTube.

## 6. SEO focus (local)
Phrases the business can plausibly rank for (volumes not measured): "airbrush painting Chennai", "hydro dipping Chennai", "bike custom paint Chromepet/Tambaram", "RX100 restoration Chennai", "airbrush training Chennai". Add Local Business structured data (address supplied; add hours when known) and a Google Business Profile (strongly recommended, none found).

## 7. Success measures
WhatsApp clicks, call taps, form submits, scroll depth to Academy, video plays. Baselines unknown.

- No hours, prices, reviews, or original media yet.
