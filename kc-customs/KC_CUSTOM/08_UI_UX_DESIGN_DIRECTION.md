# 08. UI/UX Design Direction

All three directions are built on the **extracted brand** (04): glitter purple + metallic gold on near-black, saturated bike photography, grunge/poster graphics. The logo stays untouched. Fonts are suggestions pending owner approval. No code written.

## Direction 1: Premium / Professional
- **Palette:** #12171D base, #1B2028 panels, #813D8A purple (sparingly), #B7885C gold for lines and details, off-white #ECE8F0 text.
- **Type:** elegant serif or refined grotesque headlines (e.g. Playfair Display / Inter), small caps labels.
- **Layout:** generous whitespace, large single-column showcases, thin gold dividers.
- **Hero:** slow cinematic loop of one hero build with logo, understated CTA.
- **Images:** full-bleed, even grading, minimal overlays.
- **Animation:** subtle fades and parallax.
- **UX:** calm, "gallery" feel.
- **Risk:** feels too polished/corporate against a raw, grungy, youth-driven brand. Could undersell the energy.

## Direction 2: Modern / Creative
- **Palette:** #12171D, purple gradient (#58326D → #B177C1), gold highlights, one neon accent borrowed from the logo's pink-white edge light.
- **Type:** expressive display (e.g. Syne / Clash-style) with a handwritten script accent, echoing the logo's script.
- **Layout:** asymmetric grid, overlapping cards, bento-style gallery, glitter/noise texture.
- **Hero:** split: bold headline left, vertical reel-style video right, floating sticker tags.
- **Images:** duotone purple hover states, rounded corners.
- **Animation:** scroll reveals, magnetic buttons, marquee of bike models.
- **UX:** playful, social-feed-like.
- **Risk:** can drift into the generic "creative agency" look and hurt legibility of the core message.

## Direction 3: Bold / Industry-focused (recommended)
- **Palette:** near-black **#12171D** base, **brand purple #813D8A / deep #58326D** as the dominant accent, **gold #B7885C** for fine details, **white** text. Bike colours supply all other colour. The red/yellow grunge from the RXZ posters is allowed only inside imagery.
- **Type:** heavy condensed caps for headlines (e.g. Bebas Neue / Anton) echoing the Impact-style type in their thumbnails, Inter for body, brand script only in the logo.
- **Layout:** full-width bands, thick section numbers, large "spec-sheet" cards for services, strong grid, oversized type, diagonal cuts, subtle grunge/splatter masks.
- **Hero:** full-screen muted video of an airbrushed bike in the studio (purple wall + logo), headline "Custom airbrush · Hydro dip · Builds · Chennai", two buttons: **WhatsApp your bike** and **Call**.
- **Images:** tight crops on tanks and paint detail (glitter, candy, pearl), glossy highlights, minimal overlays. A before/after slider where pairs exist.
- **Animation:** short, purposeful (clip reveals, counter for followers, hover video preview on project cards). Respect reduced-motion.
- **UX:** one-thumb mobile flow, sticky WhatsApp bar, tap-to-call, big targets, no clutter.

## Recommendation: Direction 3
Why:
1. It matches the **actual content**: loud, saturated builds, grungy poster thumbnails, Impact-style type, founder who is a bodybuilder and street-culture figure.
2. The logo's purple/gold sits well on near-black and is easy to keep intact.
3. Audience is mainly young riders on phones. Bold type and large touch targets suit that audience.
4. It stands apart from competitors found, whose sites are plain and photo-light.
5. Borrow Direction 1's gold hairlines and restraint, and Direction 2's script touch in the logo only, to stay clear of "template" looks.

**Not to do:** pastel palettes, stock-style imagery, rounded SaaS cards, gradient blobs, redesigned logo.

## Accessibility
Check purple on near-black contrast before using it for small text (use #B177C1 for text-size purple). Videos muted and captioned where speech exists. Reduced-motion fallback with a poster image.
