## 3. Craft background (general knowledge, not KC claims)
- **Hydro dipping** (water transfer printing), typical flow: prep and base coat → film floated on water → activator → dip → rinse → dry → clear coat. Source: https://www.hydrodip.com/faq/. KC's own clips visibly show the film, the dip and finished parts. Other steps are not shown, so the site only presents film → dip → finish.
- **Candy / chrome finishes**: candy is a transparent tinted layer over a metallic or chrome base, giving depth. KC shows candy (R15 V3: "candy red, candy blue") and a chrome red RX King.
- **Reverse painting**: no public source defines the term for motorcycles. KC says it is "totally different from stock paint and wrapping" (Ij2xuOjz4qI, 2.4M views) and teaches a reverse painting class. **The website will not define the technique until Chandu explains it.**
