# 04. Brand Identity (extracted, not invented)

Source: the KC Customs logo as used for the YouTube channel avatar (viewed and colour-sampled), plus four YouTube thumbnails. **No redesign. The logo is preserved exactly as the owner supplies it.**

⚠ Hex values below are **sampled from a 400 px compressed JPEG** and are approximate. Get official values or the original logo file from the owner to finalise.

## 1. Logo
- **Form:** square image. Dark background, large 3D italic **"KC"** in **purple glitter / metallic-flake texture** with a thin pink-white neon edge highlight, and **"Customs"** in **gold script** (handwritten style) overlapping the bottom right of the "C".
- **Where seen:** YouTube avatar (https://www.youtube.com/@KCcustoms09), top-right of video thumbnails, on the studio wall as a 3D sign (purple "KC", green "customs" in one thumbnail), and printed on Chandu's shirt.
- **Variations observed:** (a) dark-background square, (b) transparent/overlay version on thumbnails, (c) wall sign. A transparent PNG/SVG master has **not been located**.
- **Instagram profile picture:** not viewable. **Assumed** to match the YouTube logo.

## 3. Typography
- Logo: heavy italic geometric sans ("KC") + brush/handwritten script ("Customs"). Font family not identifiable.
- Content graphics: Impact-style heavy condensed white caps with outline/shadow ("RXZ IS AN EMOTION").
- **Recommendation (website):** a bold condensed display face for headlines (e.g. Bebas Neue / Anton), a clean sans for body (e.g. Inter), and the supplied script only inside the logo. **Do not set body text in script.** To be confirmed with the owner.

## 4. Image treatment
- Subject-first: the vehicle fills the frame, often against the purple studio wall or outdoors in green.
- Saturated, glossy, high-contrast colour. Candy/pearl paint is the hero of each shot.
- Poster composites for hero videos: grunge splatter, heavy type, founder portrait.

## 5. Video treatment
- Vertical (9:16) reels and shorts for social; horizontal (16:9) YouTube uploads.
- Titles/overlays include phone number and Chennai.
- Transitions/editing style for Instagram **cannot be assessed** (reels not accessible).

## 6. Graphic language
Grunge splatter, neon edge-light, glitter texture, metallic gold, dark background, bold type, caricature/art roots (Chandu is a caricature artist). Hashtag **#Kcstyle**.

## 7. Consistency guidelines (proposed from evidence)
1. Keep the near-black base and let the purple/gold logo and the colourful bikes provide the colour.
2. Use purple as the brand colour, gold for fine accents and details only.
3. Never recolour or restyle the logo. Place it on dark backgrounds only unless a light version is supplied.
4. The vehicle photo/video is always the loudest element.
5. Always show phone/WhatsApp, as the brand already does in every video title.

## 8. Brand personality [A, inferred]
Raw, passionate, artistic, street-credible, hands-on, youthful, proud of Tamil/Chennai roots, performance-minded (bodybuilder founder).

## 9. Tone of voice [A]
Confident, direct, enthusiastic, conversational. Real quotes from the founder: "RXZ is an emotion", "how the Brand emerged", "Best in class". Language: **English + Tamil flavour**; the founder writes casual English and Tanglish in captions. Decision on Tamil pending (see `10`).
