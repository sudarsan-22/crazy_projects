## Content patterns
- **Titles always carry the brand, "Chennai" and the phone number** (9677184462). Very strong, consistent call to action.
- Dominant subject: **Yamaha RX-series restoration/custom**. The most-viewed items (9.3K, 8K, 6.9K) are RXZ, RX115 and RX100.
- Educational/process content exists ("full process", "classes").
- Community content (Tamil gana songs for Chandu) is **not business content**. Excluding it from the website is recommended.
