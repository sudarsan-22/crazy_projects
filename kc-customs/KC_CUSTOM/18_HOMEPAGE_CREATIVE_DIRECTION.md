# 18. Homepage Creative Direction

## Concept: "The Studio Wall"
KC's new studio has walls covered in painted panels and helmets. The site works the same way: tall 9:16 panels of real work, lit purple on near-black, which the visitor walks past and opens. The brand's own line, **"Beyond the Expectations"**, is the headline.
