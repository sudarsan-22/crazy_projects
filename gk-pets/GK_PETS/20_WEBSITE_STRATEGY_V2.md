# 20 – Website Strategy V2

## Evidence summary
Confirmed: bird-focused storefront (11 categories), "Wholesale Dealer of Birds, Aquarium Feeds & Accessories" on printed material, old DotPe store with UPI-only payment, no returns, free delivery, PAN-India shipping claim, two addresses (shop + gowdown), bilingual (Tamil/English) logo. Unconfirmed: wholesale vs retail share, MOQ, importing, live birds, fish range, prices, social accounts, whether gkpets.in is still live.

## Concept A – Wholesale-focused product catalogue
- Category catalogue (Birds food, hand-feeding, accessories, cages, aquarium feeds…) with brand pages, pack sizes, "Request price list"/"Enquire on WhatsApp" per product, MOQ section, dealer enquiry form, downloadable catalogue, visit-us (shop + gowdown) page.
- No cart/payments. Tamil + English.
- Fit with evidence: matches printed "Wholesale Dealer" tagline and Facebook description; competitors in this niche have weak sites (see 18). Low maintenance.
- Risk: loses retail UPI buyers the old store served.

## Concept B – Retail + wholesale hybrid pet-supplies site
- Everything in A plus public retail prices, cart, UPI/online payments, order tracking, delivery rules, returns policy, wholesale login/price tiers.
- Fit: the old DotPe store (retail ordering, free delivery, PAN India) shows retail intent; MyOwnGarden shows the retail benchmark.
- Risk: needs live prices, stock, logistics, GST and support; storefront data had 0 loaded products and a no-returns policy; unclear whether retail ever traded online.

## Comparison
| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Matches verified positioning (wholesale) | Strong | Partial |
| Evidence of online retail demand | – | Weak (old store, no sales data) |
| Cost/complexity | Low | High |
| Competitive gap | Clear | Crowded (retailers) |
| Can scale to B later | Yes | – |

## Recommendation
**Tentative: A (wholesale catalogue with WhatsApp enquiry), leaving a path to B.** The business model is **not sufficiently verified** to commit: answers to questionnaire items 5, 14, 15, 16 and 23 decide. If the owner says retail online sales are a goal, choose B. No design/implementation started.
