## 3. Tamil-specific discovery

### 3.1 Search terms for Instagram's audio picker (🔵)
| Goal | Try searching |
|---|---|
| Mass / hero | `mass bgm`, `<hero name> bgm`, `<film name> bgm`, `theme`, `intro bgm` |
| Villain / thriller | `villain bgm`, `<film> villain theme`, `suspense bgm tamil` |
| Comedy dialogue | `vadivelu`, `goundamani`, `senthil`, `santhanam`, `yogi babu`, `vivek`, `<film> comedy` |
| Sad / tragedy | `sad bgm tamil`, `emotional bgm`, `violin bgm` |
| Nostalgia | `ilaiyaraaja`, `90s tamil`, `harris jayaraj`, `yuvan bgm`, `rahman melody` |
| Kuthu / dance | `kuthu`, `<composer> kuthu`, `gaana` |
| New releases | the **film name** of recent Tamil releases ([Wikipedia: Tamil films of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tamil_films_of_2026)) |

### 3.2 Leading indicators outside Instagram
| Signal | Why | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **New Tamil film releases** (songs, trailers, dialogue) | Trailer dialogues and lead songs commonly spike after release 🔵 | Verify on Instagram before calling it trending |
| **YouTube Shorts (Tamil)** | **TikTok has been banned in India since June 2020**, so Shorts is the main parallel short-video platform for Tamil audiences. Sounds often surface there too 🔵 | Different algorithm |
| **Spotify playlists**: e.g. "Instagram Trending Tamil Hits" (Filtr India) | Curators compile Reels-popular Tamil tracks ([Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/5E4lCK9QDI0K9AIFdjNvwz)) | User- or editor-curated. Not Instagram data |
| **YouTube "Reels trending songs" playlists** | Community compilations | Low reliability. Candidates only |
| **Global trend reports** (Buffer / Metricool weekly) | Good for English audio trends ([Buffer Nov 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/trending-audio-instagram/), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/trending-instagram-songs/)) | US-centric. Check whether it's trending in India |

## 4. Evidence standard for audio claims

| Claim | Minimum evidence required | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| **Audio name / source film / composer** | Audio page screenshot, or a reliable public source (label, Wikipedia, film credits) | "Source not confirmed" |
| **Usage count** | **Audio page screenshot with the date** | `UNVERIFIED — needs screenshot`. Never estimated |
| **"Trending"** | ↗ arrow visible in a dated screenshot, or listed in the Trending leaderboard screenshot | "Trend status unknown" |
| **Growing / declining** | **Two dated counts** (≥2 days apart) → velocity, **or** dated top-Reels ages (recent top Reels = active) | "Direction unknown" |
| **Successful example Reels** | Screenshot or link of Reels on the audio page with visible views or likes | "No examples verified" |
| **Fit for my video** | My analysis (labelled opinion) | — |

### 4.1 Trend lifecycle (classification rules, no invented thresholds)
| Stage | Observable signals | Use it? |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Emerging** | Count small relative to similar audios. Growing fast between two checks. Top Reels mostly from the last few days. ↗ may or may not show | ✅ Best window if it fits. Higher upside, less proven |
| 📈 **Rising** | ↗ visible. Count growing clearly day to day. Top Reels recent | ✅ Prime window |
| 🏔️ **Peak / saturated** | Very high count. Growth slowing. Many near-identical Reels in your feed | ⚠️ Only with a genuinely new twist |
| 📉 **Declining** | Little growth between checks. Top Reels are weeks old | ❌ Unless it's evergreen-style |
| ♾️ **Evergreen** | Classic BGM or comedy dialogue used steadily for years (e.g. iconic comedy tracks) | ✅ Any time. The joke matters, not timing |

**Velocity formula** (once two dated counts exist): `growth per day = (count₂ − count₁) ÷ days between`. Compare candidates **against each other**, not against a magic number.

## 5. Screenshot protocol (what to send me)

For each candidate audio (or when you find something interesting):
1. **Audio page top:** name, artist or film, **Reel count**, the ↗ arrow if any
2. **First 6–9 top Reels** visible with their view counts
3. **Date of the screenshot** (the filename is fine: `AUDIO_<shortname>_2026-11-12.png`)
4. **Optional second screenshot 2–3 days later** → growth direction
5. Your **Trending leaderboard** screenshot once a week (top 50)

Save to: `account-data/audio/` · Log entries go into [account-data/audio-log.md](account-data/audio-log.md).

## 6. Weekly audio scouting routine (≈15 min, you + me)

| Step | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You | Screenshot the **Trending leaderboard** + save interesting audios while scrolling (bookmark) |
| 2 | You | Search 3–4 Tamil terms from §3.1. Screenshot promising audio pages |
| 3 | Me | Web check: source film or composer, release date, streaming signals, any licensing issues ([15 §6](15-tamil-music-and-audio-strategy.md#6-licensing--legal-india-specific)) |
| 4 | Me | Log each audio in [audio-log.md](account-data/audio-log.md) with stage (§4.1) and suggested **role** ([15 §3](15-tamil-music-and-audio-strategy.md#3-audio-role-library-choose-by-the-job-not-the-song)) |
| 5 | Both | Re-check counts on promising ones 2–3 days later → direction |

Result: a **living audio library**, so that when you upload a clip, matching ([27](27-audio-matching-framework.md)) starts from verified candidates rather than guesses.

---

## 7. Per-clip audio research (when you upload footage)

1. I analyse the clip and decide which **audio roles** fit (hero, villain, comedy, nostalgia…) ([26 §3](26-video-production-workflow.md#3-stage-1--footage-analysis))
2. I pull candidates from: **(a)** the audio log (verified), **(b)** evergreen Tamil classics for that role, **(c)** web research on recent releases and English trends
3. I score them with [27](27-audio-matching-framework.md). Popularity is scored **only** where evidence exists
4. I send you a **verification list**: "please screenshot these 5 audio pages"
5. After your screenshots, I finalise the comparison and recommend **one**. You approve before any editing
