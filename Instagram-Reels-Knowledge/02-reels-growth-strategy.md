# 02 — Reels Growth Strategy

## 1. The growth model

Growth on Reels runs as a funnel. Each stage has its own job and its own metric.

```
REACH  (non-followers see it)        → metric: non-follower reach, skip rate
  ↓
RETAIN (they watch it through)       → metric: avg watch time ÷ length, retention graph
  ↓
REACT  (they send/save/like)         → metric: sends/reach, saves/reach, likes/reach
  ↓
CONVERT (they follow)                → metric: follows/reach, profile visits → follows
  ↓
RETURN (followers keep watching)     → metric: follower reach %, repeat engagement
```

🔵 Most stalled accounts are broken at one stage. Find the weakest stage in [09 — Analytics](09-instagram-analytics.md) and fix that first.

## 2. Positioning: niche, audience, promise

Recommendation systems match content to **interest clusters** ✅ (see [01](01-instagram-algorithm.md#2-how-reels-are-ranked-the-pipeline)). A clear niche makes you easy to classify, and easy for a viewer to decide to follow.

### Define these before Phase 4 (fill in during Phase 2)

| Element | Question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience** | Who exactly? | "Beginner video editors editing on their phones" |
| **Problem / desire** | What do they want? | "Make Reels look professional without expensive gear" |
| **Promise** (bio line) | Why follow? | "Pro-level phone edits in 30 seconds" |
| **Angle** | Why you, and what's different? | "I show the before/after of every edit" |
| **Proof** | Why believe you? | Results, credentials, portfolio, consistency |

🔵 **The follow test:** If a stranger sees one Reel and then your grid, can they tell in 3 seconds what they'll get by following? If not, conversion will be weak however viral one Reel gets.

### Content pillars (3–4)

🔵 Pick 3–4 recurring themes that all serve the same audience:

| Pillar type | Goal | Share of content |
|---|---|---|
| **Teach / value** (tutorials, tips, breakdowns) | Saves, sends, authority | ~40% |
| **Relate / entertain** (POV, humour, "editor problems") | Sends, reach | ~30% |
| **Show / proof** (before-after, results, behind the scenes) | Trust, follows | ~20% |
| **Personal / opinion** (story, hot takes, journey) | Connection, comments | ~10% |

## 3. Formats that perform in 2026

🔵 These are formats that keep recurring across successful creators in 2026. They're structures, not trends, so they don't expire quickly.

| Format | Structure | Why it works | Main metric it drives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before → After reveal** | Show the end result for 0.5 s, then "here's how", then the process, then the full reveal | Curiosity plus payoff | Watch-through, saves |
| **Fast tutorial / "how to X in Y seconds"** | Promise, steps (1 per 2–4 s), result | Useful and saveable | Saves, sends |
| **Listicle ("3 mistakes…")** | Number in the hook, items, best one last | Built-in progress bar keeps people watching | Watch time |
| **POV / relatable skit** | Text: "POV: you're an editor and the client says…" | Viewers recognise themselves | **Sends** |
| **Myth-busting / contrarian** | "Stop doing X" and why, then what to do instead | Pattern break, debate | Comments, sends |
| **Talking head + B-roll** | Face for trust, B-roll for each claim | Personal and visual | Follows |
| **Green-screen commentary** | React to an article, post or screenshot with your own analysis | Counts as transformative (original) | Comments |
| **Process / satisfying** | Silent or ASMR process, timelapse, no talking | Replays, universal (no language barrier) | Replays, watch time |
| **Storytime** | In-medias-res opening, then tension, then twist or lesson | Open loop retention | Watch-through |
| **Series ("Part 3 of…")** | Recurring numbered format | Gives a reason to follow | Follows |
| **Day-in-the-life / BTS** | Montage with voiceover | Parasocial connection | Follows, comments |
| **Remix / reply-to-comment video** | Answer a real comment on screen | Community, built-in relevance | Comments |

✅ **Carousels still matter.** The originality policy now treats photos and carousels like Reels, and Feed recommends them too. 🔵 Carousels are a strong second format for saves (step-by-step guides, cheat sheets).

🟡 **Watch for "Series":** Instagram is testing a feature that groups related Reels into a profile hub with a "Watch Next" button ([SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)). If it launches, a series format becomes a structural advantage.

## 4. Trend usage, done right

- 🔵 **Trends get attention. Your niche is what keeps it.** Use a trending audio or format **only** if you can fit it to your pillar. A trend outside your niche brings viewers who won't follow.
- ✅ Going to the audio page is a ranking prediction, so audio that inspires others to create carries a little extra weight.
- 🔵 **Find trends:** the Reels tab (arrow ↗ next to the audio means trending), the Professional Dashboard's trending audio (where available), the Edits app's inspiration and ideas feed, and competitor audits (see [09](09-instagram-analytics.md#5-analysing-successful-creators-teardown-method)).
- 🔵 **Timing:** A trending audio usually peaks within days to a couple of weeks. Being early helps. Being late still works if your take is original.
- ⚠️ **Business accounts** can't use most popular licensed music (see [06](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#3-licensing-what-you-can-legally-use)). Plan trend participation around that.

## 5. Testing system (Trial Reels + iteration)

✅ Trial Reels go only to non-followers. Use them as your lab.

**Weekly loop (🔵 practitioner framework):**
1. **Hypothesis:** "A text hook naming the mistake will beat a visual-only hook."
2. **Change one variable:** hook, length, format, caption, cover, or audio.
3. **Run it as a Trial Reel**, or two variants on separate days.
4. **Compare at the same age** (for example 72 h): skip rate, avg watch %, sends/reach, follows/reach.
5. **Keep the winner, kill the loser, write the lesson** in the performance log (Phase 5).

🔵 **Repackaging winners:** A Reel that hit can be **re-edited**, with a new hook, a different first shot and a tighter cut, and posted again weeks later. Change it meaningfully. Don't repost an identical file, because duplicate detection exists ✅.

## 6. Collaboration and community

| Tactic | How | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Collab posts** | Invite a co-author. The post shows on both profiles and to both audiences | ✅ Instagram's recommended way to co-create |
| **Remix** | Respond to another creator's Reel side by side | ✅ Treated as original contribution |
| **Reply-to-comment Reels** | Turn a comment into the next video | 🔵 |
| **DM/Story sharing** | Share others' Reels to your Story rather than reposting to the feed | ✅ Instagram recommends Story share for others' Reels |
| **Broadcast channels** | Deeper engagement with core fans | 🔵 |
| **Reply to comments in the first hour** | Builds community. Comment threads are engagement | 🔵 Good practice. ⚠️ Not a proven "boost hack" |

## 7. Converting viewers into followers

🔵 Reach without follows is wasted. Optimise the profile as the landing page.

- **Name field:** Your name plus a keyword (for example "Asha | Reels Editing Tips"). ✅ The name field is searchable text.
- **Bio:** Audience, promise and proof in 150 characters, plus one CTA.
- **Profile photo:** A clear face, high contrast, recognisable at a small size.
- **Highlights:** "Start here", tutorials, results.
- **Pinned posts (up to 3):** Your best "who I am / what you get" Reels and your biggest hits.
- **Grid consistency:** 🔵 Covers with consistent text placement and style. ✅ The grid shows a **3:4** crop (changed January 2025), so design covers for it ([07](07-instagram-seo-and-optimization.md#6-covers--thumbnails)).
- **Series hooks:** "Follow for part 2" only works when part 2 is real and worth it.

## 8. Posting cadence and consistency

- ✅ Instagram has **never published a required frequency**.
- 🔵 / 🟡 Common guidance: **3–5 Reels per week** if quality holds, plus Stories for follower connection. Mosseri has said in the past that posting more gives more chances to break through, but **quality beats volume**.
- 🔵 **Pick a cadence you can keep for 90 days.** Consistency builds the data you need for analytics, and the habit.
- 🔵 **Batch production** (see [10](10-content-creation-workflow.md)) is how solo creators sustain 3–5 a week.

---

## 9. What NOT to do (growth)

- ❌ Buy followers, likes or views, or use bots, follow/unfollow, engagement pods or comment groups. These violate Instagram's guidelines, wreck your audience signals (fake followers never watch, so your ratios fall), and can restrict the account.
- ❌ Repost others' content as your main strategy. Since April 2026 it makes the account ineligible for recommendations ✅.
- ❌ Chase every trend outside your niche.
- ❌ Post 3+ minute Reels when you want non-follower reach ✅.
- ❌ Pivot topics every week. The classifier and your audience both get confused.
- ❌ Engagement bait ("comment YES if…" with no real reason). Mosseri has warned against forcing shares or engagement. Ask for actions only when there's a genuine reason.
