## Evidence legend (used in every document)

Instagram doesn't publish its ranking formula, and blogs often present guesses as fact, so each claim is tagged:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Official** | Stated by Instagram/Meta: Help Center, Creators blog, about.instagram.com, Meta newsroom/transparency, or Adam Mosseri on his own channels |
| 🟡 **Reported** | Credible press or widely repeated summaries of official statements. I couldn't check it against the primary source, or reports disagree on details |
| 🔵 **Practitioner** | Craft knowledge from professional editors and creators. It's about technique, not the algorithm, and it's well established |
| ⚠️ **Theory** | Creator speculation, unverified, or contradicted by official statements. **Do not build strategy on these** |
| 🧪 **Tested here** | Run and measured on this machine (docs 21–34). For software docs (29–34), ✅ also covers project documentation, PyPI metadata and GitHub data |

## The 10 things that matter most (TL;DR)

1. ✅ **Reels is a discovery surface.** Most Reels views come from people who don't follow you. Make every Reel understandable to a stranger.
2. ✅ **Instagram predicts:** will they watch to the end, like it, reshare it, and open the audio page? Edit for **watch-through** and **sendability**.
3. 🟡 **Mosseri's top three signals:** watch time, likes and sends. **Sends per reach** matters most for reaching non-followers.
4. ✅ **Originality is enforced.** Reposts, aggregations, watermarks, borders and low-effort edits lose recommendation eligibility. Since 30 April 2026 this covers photos and carousels too.
5. ✅ **Recommendation eligibility:** public account, Reels of **3 minutes or less**, no Recommendation Guideline issues. Check **Settings → Account Status** regularly.
6. ✅ **Hook in the first 3 seconds.** That's Instagram's own advice. The new **Skip rate** metric measures exactly this.
7. ✅ **Search is text-based.** Put keywords in your name field, bio and caption. Hashtags are capped at **5** per post (🟡 Dec 2025) and help categorisation, not reach.
8. 🔵 **Export 1080×1920, 9:16, H.264 MP4, 30 fps (60 for action), and turn on "Upload at highest quality."** Keep text inside the centre safe zone.
9. ✅ **Use Trial Reels** to test hooks and formats on non-followers without affecting your followers' feeds.
10. ✅ **Judge Reels by ratios, not raw views:** skip rate, average watch time ÷ length, sends/reach, saves/reach, follows/reach, and the share of reach that came from non-followers.
