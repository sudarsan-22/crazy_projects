## 1. Choosing your editing stack

| Your situation | Recommended stack |
|---|---|
| Beginner, phone only, free | **Edits** (Instagram's app) + CapCut free (no watermark export) |
| Serious mobile creator | **Edits** or **CapCut Pro** + **LumaFusion** (iOS/Android) or **Final Cut Pro for iPad** |
| Desktop, free, wants pro skills | **DaVinci Resolve (free)** |
| Desktop, pro, Adobe ecosystem | **Premiere Pro** + After Effects + Adobe Podcast |
| Mac-only, speed-focused | **Final Cut Pro** |
| Talking-head / podcast heavy | **Descript** (edit by transcript) → finish in Resolve, Premiere or CapCut |
| Repurposing long videos | **OpusClip** or similar clipper → polish in an editor |

🔵 **Recommendation for this project:** Learn **one desktop editor deeply** (DaVinci Resolve if budget matters, Premiere Pro if you want the Adobe ecosystem) **plus Edits** for quick, Instagram-native work. Pros get their speed from mastering one tool, not from owning many.

## 4. AI tools (by job)

| Job | Tools | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto-captions & styled subtitles** | Edits, CapCut, Premiere, Resolve Studio, **Submagic**, Captions app | 🟡 Caption features have converged. Choose by style and price ([comparison](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-ai-video-editors)) |
| **Long → short clipping** | **OpusClip**, Vizard, Reap, Descript | Good for finding moments. Always re-edit the hook manually |
| **Silence & filler removal** | Descript, Edits "First Draft" (🟡 auto-trims pauses), Premiere text-based editing, AutoCut, FireCut | Saves hours on talking heads |
| **Voice enhancement** | Adobe Podcast Enhance, Resolve Voice Isolation, Premiere Enhance Speech, Auphonic | Use at 50–80% to avoid robotic artefacts |
| **Upscale / slow-mo / denoise** | **Topaz Video AI**, Resolve Super Scale and Speed Warp | Rescues old or low-light footage |
| **Generative video / extend / B-roll** | Adobe Firefly / Generative Extend, Runway, Google Veo, Kling, Pika | ✅ Label realistic AI visuals ("AI info"). ✅ AI-persona accounts must label (Aug 2026) 🟡 |
| **Masking / rotoscoping** | Resolve Magic Mask, Premiere Object Mask, Runway, CapCut Remove BG | "Text behind subject" effects |
| **Scripting & ideation** | General LLM assistants, Edits weekly ideas (🟡 10 ideas per week) | Use for brainstorming and outlines. Keep your own voice and facts |
| **AI voiceover** | ElevenLabs and similar | ⚠️ Disclose synthetic voices. Never clone a voice without consent |
| **AI music** | Various generators | ⚠️ Check commercial licence terms and ownership carefully |

🔵 **AI principle:** Use AI to **remove grunt work** (captions, silence, noise, rough cuts). Keep **creative decisions** (hook, story, pacing, payoff) human. That's what makes content original ✅ and worth watching.

## 6. Presets, templates, LUTs & plugins

### LUTs & colour
- **Free:** Blackmagic's built-in LUTs, manufacturer log-to-709 LUTs (Apple Log, Sony, Panasonic, DJI). Many free creator LUT packs exist (check the licence).
- **Paid / pro:** Dehancer (film emulation), FilmConvert Nitrate, Colorist Factory, Juan Melara's Resolve PowerGrades.
- 🔵 Always apply a LUT **after** correction, at reduced intensity.

### Transitions & effects
- **Premiere:** Film Impact (transitions), Red Giant Universe, Boris FX Sapphire / Continuum, Mister Horse Animation Composer (After Effects).
- **Resolve:** Built-in Fusion templates, MrAlexTech and other Fusion packs, Boris FX.
- **Motion blur / speed:** ReelSmart Motion Blur, Twixtor (RE:Vision).
- **Tracking:** Mocha Pro (Boris FX).

### Templates & assets (stock)
- **Envato Elements**, **Motion Array**, **Artlist** (footage, templates, motion graphics), **Mixkit** (free), **Pexels / Pixabay** (free stock footage).
- **Edits / CapCut templates:** fast, but avoid looking like everyone else. Customise heavily.

### Fonts
- **Google Fonts** (free, commercial-OK): Montserrat, Inter, Poppins, Bebas Neue, Anton, DM Sans, Playfair Display.
- 🔵 Check the font licence for commercial use before using it in brand work.

## 7. Hardware essentials (budget → pro)

| Item | Budget | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Recent smartphone (4K60, log if available) | Mirrorless (Sony ZV-E10 II / FX30, Canon R50/R10, Lumix S5II) or DJI Osmo Pocket 3-class |
| Audio | Wired lav (~$20) | Wireless lav kit (DJI Mic / Rode Wireless / Hollyland) |
| Light | Window light plus a reflector | 1–2 LED panels or a COB light with a softbox |
| Stabilisation | Tripod plus phone clamp | Gimbal (DJI Osmo Mobile / RS series) |
| Editing machine | Any modern laptop with 16 GB RAM | Apple Silicon Mac or a PC with a modern GPU, 32 GB RAM, NVMe SSD |
| Monitoring | Earbuds | Closed-back headphones (Sony MDR-7506 / ATH-M50x class) |
