## Workflow: what happens when you send a Reel

1. **You:** drop the file in `account-data/reels/` (for example `R03_2026-10-31.mp4`) and fill its block in [account-data/reel-intake.md](account-data/reel-intake.md). Add Insights screenshots when you have them.
2. **Me: measure.** Run [tools/analyze_reel.py](tools/README.md), which measures format, loudness, audio start, black frames, cuts per 10 s, shot lengths and cut-to-beat sync, and extracts the hook frames, safe-zone overlays, contact sheet and transition strips.
3. **Me: observe.** Review the extracted frames and build a timestamped shot log (Part A2 of the audit).
4. **Me: judge.** Score the 12 criteria with the rubric (§C1), citing evidence.
5. **Me: recommend.** Keep / fix / test, specific to that Reel.
6. **Me: roll up.** Update this document: the tracker, comparison tables (§A4, §B1, §C2), the synthesis (§C3), recommendations (§D) and the update log.

**My analysis limits:**
- I analyse **sampled frames plus measurements**, not live playback. Comic timing and lip-sync tightness are judged from frame strips and your notes, and labelled as opinion.
- I **can't identify songs by ear or transcribe Tamil speech** with the tools installed now. Audio names come from your intake notes. (Transcription is possible with an extra tool; I'll ask before installing anything.)

## Part C — Editorial opinions

> Everything here is **my judgement**, not fact. Each opinion cites evidence from Part A or B.

### C1. Scoring rubric (1–5)
General scale: **1** absent or hurts the Reel · **2** weak · **3** competent · **4** strong · **5** signature-level, a benchmark for future Reels.

| # | Criterion | 1 looks like… | 3 looks like… | 5 looks like… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First 3-s hook | Black or slow start, no reason to stay | Clear subject and text by ~1.5 s | Visual + text + audio hook stacked in under 1 s. Stranger-proof. Curiosity |
| 2 | Concept & storytelling | No clear idea | One clear idea with an ending | Setup → escalation → payoff or twist. Rewatchable |
| 3 | Audio selection | Random or generic bed | Fits the mood | Audio *tells* part of the joke. Clear role ([15 §3](15-tamil-music-and-audio-strategy.md#3-audio-role-library-choose-by-the-job-not-the-song)). Fresh use of a familiar track |
| 4 | Audio sync | Cuts ignore the audio | Main moments land on beats | Frame-accurate. Switches and drops land on the payoff. Tight lip-sync |
| 5 | Cuts & transitions | Dead air, long static holds, preset gimmicks | Tight, motivated cuts | Invisible or signature transitions with SFX. Deliberate pacing contrast |
| 6 | Camera & composition | Shaky, badly framed, subject under the UI | Stable, clear subject, inside the safe zone | Deliberate angles (low hero, pet eye-level), depth, variety |
| 7 | Colour | Bad exposure or white balance, inconsistent | Correct and consistent | An intentional look serving the mood (MovieMode vs Reality) |
| 8 | Text & typography | Unreadable, typos, under the UI | Readable, timed, in the safe zone | Styled, animated with purpose, consistent with your brand |
| 9 | Entertainment value | No reaction | A smile | A laugh, or an "I have to send this" reflex |
| 10 | Retention potential *(prediction)* | Likely swiped within 3 s | Holds to the middle | An open loop holds to the end, and the ending invites a replay |
| 12 | Share/save potential *(prediction)* | No reason to share | Mildly relatable | Strong "this is so you" or a useful reference |

*(Criterion 11, editing improvement opportunities, is a list rather than a score.)*
