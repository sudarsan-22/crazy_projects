## 1. Why Tamil audio is a strong identity

| Reason | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared cultural vocabulary** | Tamil cinema dialogue and BGM work as a common language. Viewers instantly recognise a "mass entry", a "sad violin" or a Vadivelu line | 🔵 / 🟡 Vadivelu has been called "the spine of Tamil Nadu's meme culture" ([Film Companion](https://www.filmcompanion.in/features/tamil-features/vadivelu-the-man-from-the-meme-maamannan-udhayanidhi-stalin-tamil-comedy-mari-selvaraj)) |
| **Instant context** | One second of a famous BGM sets the genre (hero, villain, tragedy, romance) with no explanation needed. That's perfect for 3-second hooks | 🔵 |
| **Sendability** | In-group humour gets shared with friends who'll "get it" ✅. Sends are a key signal for reaching non-followers | ✅ / 🟡 |
| **Audio page discovery** | ✅ Instagram predicts whether viewers will open the audio page. Popular Tamil audios have browsable pages where your Reel can be found | ✅ |
| **Proven creator format** | Tamil creators have built audiences on music-led comedy. ## 2. The five ways music creates comedy, emotion and surprise

These are the core techniques. Every Tamil-audio Reel you make should use at least one deliberately.

### 2.1 Contrast (epic music × tiny moment): **your signature**
Mass hero BGM plus slow motion plus a freeze-frame name card… for taking the rabbit's food bowl to the kitchen.
- **Why it's funny:** the gap between how important the music says this is and how unimportant it actually is
- **Execution:** commit fully. Proper slow-mo (60/120 fps), low hero angle, dramatic grade. The more serious the treatment, the funnier it is

### 2.2 The BGM switch (expectation → reality)
Epic or romantic music builds up, then a **hard cut, record scratch or silence**, then a comedy track or sad BGM as the reality hits.
- **Timing:** the switch lands on the exact frame of the fail. 0.3–0.5 s of silence before the switch makes it hit harder ([06 §4](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#4-sound-design-layering-sfx))
- **Best for:** DIY fails, stupid purchases, "me vs my pets", R15 or Verna moments

### 2.3 Dialogue dub / lip-sync over your own life
A famous Tamil movie or comedy dialogue performed (lip-synced or acted) by **you**, or mapped onto your pets or vehicle, in a new situation.
- **Classic source types:** Vadivelu, Goundamani–Senthil, Vivek, Santhanam, Yogi Babu comedy tracks; iconic hero punch dialogues; villain lines ([Citizen Matters on Chennai meme culture](https://citizenmatters.in/growth-of-meme-culture-in-chennai/))
- **The twist that makes it yours:** the *situation* is new and specific to your life (the rabbit ignoring you, the Verna's fuel light, your parents seeing an Amazon parcel)
- **Precision:** lip-sync must be frame-accurate. Cut on syllables, not approximately

### 2.4 Genre casting (music as character)
Assign a **recurring theme** to each "character" in your life:
| Character | Audio role |
|---|---|
| You | The "struggling hero" (comedy BGM / underdog theme) |
| Rabbit | **Villain theme** (menacing BGM for a fluffy rabbit) |
| Birds | Gossip or comedy track / romantic melody |
| R15 | Mass hero entry BGM |
| Verna | Stylish or classy BGM, or "family car" melody |
| DIY project | Training-montage or motivational BGM → tragedy BGM if it fails |

🔵 Repeated character themes become **running jokes**. By Reel 5, followers expect the villain BGM when the rabbit appears. That's recognition, and it's a reason to follow.

### 2.5 Nostalgia and emotion (the "Feel" 20%)
Soft melodies for drives, early-morning rides, pet bonding and room glow-ups.
- 🔵 Common associations (stylistic shorthand, not rules): **Ilaiyaraaja** (70s–90s nostalgia, village and road melodies), **A.R. Rahman** (90s–2000s emotion), **Harris Jayaraj** and **Yuvan Shankar Raja** (2000s college or road-trip nostalgia, emotional BGMs), **Anirudh** (modern energy and mass), **Santhosh Narayanan** (raw, rooted), **D. Imman** (folk or rural melody)
- **Add one funny line of text** even in emotional Reels, so it stays "you", not a generic sad edit

## 3. Audio role library (choose by the job, not the song)

Build this as a personal swipe file (Phase 3). For each role, save 3–5 audios from the **Instagram audio library** (tap the audio → Save).

| Role | Use when | Edit pairing |
|---|---|---|
| 🦸 **Mass hero entry** | Introducing anything trivial dramatically | Slow-mo walk-in, low angle, freeze frame plus name card |
| 😈 **Villain theme** | The pets, the alarm clock, a traffic jam, the electricity bill | Push-in on the "villain", dark grade |
| 😭 **Tragedy / sad violin** | Fails, broken DIY, empty fuel tank | Slow push-in, desaturate, rain or blue tint |
| 🤣 **Comedy track / comedian dialogue** | Reactions, POVs, reality checks | Hard cuts, punch-ins on the reaction |
| 💕 **Romantic melody** | You and your R15, you and the new gadget, the birds | Soft focus, warm grade, slow motion |
| 🕵️ **Suspense / thriller** | "Who ate the cable?" (rabbit), a missing remote | Quick cuts, tension SFX, a reveal on the drop |
| 🏋️ **Training montage** | DIY build process, room setup grind | Fast timelapse cuts on the beat |
| 🌅 **Nostalgic melody** | Road trips, morning rides, old photos | Long smooth shots, warm film grade |
| 🔥 **Trending Tamil song** | Trend formats with your twist | Beat-synced transitions |
| 🌍 **English trending audio** | Universal visual gags (pets, gadgets), global trends | Per the trend's format |

## 4. Finding trending Tamil audio (weekly routine, 15 minutes)

✅ / 🔵 No reliable external ranking exists, so use Instagram itself:
1. **Reels feed:** an **↗ arrow** next to the audio name means it's trending. Tap it to see the Reel count and how recent the top Reels are.
2. **Audio page check:** look at how many Reels use it and whether the top Reels are **recent** (this week means it's rising; months old means it's peaking or declining).
3. **Professional dashboard / Edits app:** trending audio and inspiration sections (availability varies) 🟡.
4. **Tamil cinema calendar:** new songs, trailers and dialogues spike around **film releases** (big festival releases at Pongal, Tamil New Year, Diwali). Track upcoming releases ([Wikipedia: List of Tamil films of 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tamil_films_of_2026)). Trailer dialogues often trend for 1–2 weeks after release.
5. **Spotify / YouTube Tamil charts:** songs climbing streaming charts usually become Reels audio soon after.
6. **Save to the swipe file** with a note: role · date found · Reel count.

**Timing rule (🔵):** Use a trending audio **within its first 1–2 weeks** for momentum. After that, only use it if your twist is genuinely new. Evergreen classics (comedy dialogues, nostalgic songs) work any time.

## 5. Matching music with edits (technical)

| Technique | How | Cross-ref |
|---|---|---|
| **Beat-mapped cuts** | Mark beats first, then cut transitions on downbeats | [06 §7](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#7-beat-syncing) |
| **Drop = reveal** | The visual payoff (glow-up, pet entry, bike reveal) lands on the musical drop | [04 §3](04-professional-editing-techniques.md#3-speed-ramping--slow-motion) |
| **Speed ramp to the beat** | Ramp slow into the hit and fast out of it, so the action lands on the beat | [04 §3](04-professional-editing-techniques.md#3-speed-ramping--slow-motion) |
| **Silence before the punchline** | Cut all audio 0.3–0.5 s before the switch or reveal | [06 §4](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#4-sound-design-layering-sfx) |
| **Dialogue-first timing** | For dialogue Reels, lay the dialogue first and cut video to the syllables | §2.3 |
| **Start at the iconic part** | Begin the audio at its most recognisable second (the hook of the song or BGM), not its intro | [06 §2](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#2-background-music-selection) |
| **Mixed audio** | Your real sound (laughs, rabbit thumps, engine) layered with the BGM keeps it authentic | 🔵 |

### Licensed-audio workflow (important)

✅ Music added **inside Instagram** is licensed through Meta's agreements and links your Reel to the audio page. Music baked into your export file is neither.

**Option A: edit inside Edits (recommended for Tamil library audio).** 🟡 Edits has access to Instagram's audio library, so you can cut to the exact song with licensing and audio-page linking intact.

**Option B: desktop editor (Resolve or Premiere) for complex edits:**
1. Use the song in your editor as a **timing guide** only
2. Export with the music **muted** (keep your SFX, dialogue and natural sound)
3. In Instagram or Edits, add the same song from the library and choose the **same start point**
4. Fine-tune the sync and adjust the original-audio vs music balance

## 6. Licensing & legal (India-specific)

| Situation | Status | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Personal or creator Reel using a Tamil film song **from Instagram's library** | Generally covered by Meta's licences with labels (Meta has deals with Indian labels including **Saregama** (2020) and, per reports, **T-Series, Zee Music, Yash Raj**) for **personal, non-commercial use** | ✅ / 🟡 ([TechCrunch 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/02/facebook-strikes-global-licensing-deal-with-indian-music-label-saregama)) |
| **Paid promotion or brand collab** using library film songs | **Not covered.** In 2026 **Zee sued Nykaa** for ~₹2 crore over 12 branded Reels that used Zee songs from Instagram's library. The reels were taken down. The case was pending as of July 2026 | 🟡 ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/biz-tech/2026/05/21/nykaa-vs-zee-music-the-instagram-reels-lawsuit-that-could-change-things-for-small-creators.html), [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/05/223-zee-nykaa-delhi-high-court-copyrighted-songs-instagram-reels/)) |
| Songs downloaded from YouTube or Spotify and **baked into the video** | Not licensed. Risk of muting, blocking or takedown | ✅ |
| **Movie video clips** (scenes, meme footage) reposted | Fails the **originality** rules, so no recommendations, plus copyright risk | ✅ ([01 §5](01-instagram-algorithm.md#5-originality-rules-2024--2026)) |
| **Ilaiyaraaja compositions** | Legally contested territory. He has won court orders over the use of his songs (for example the *Good Bad Ugly* case, Madras HC 2025, settled for ₹50 lakh in Dec 2025). No Instagram-specific action found, but availability in the library could change | 🟡 ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/madras-high-court/madras-high-court-ilaiyaraaja-mythri-movie-makers-suit-settled-312183)) |

### Practical rules
1. ✅ **Always add film audio from Instagram's library**, never as a downloaded file.
2. ✅ **Your footage, their audio** is the safe and original formula.
3. 🔵 **Recreate meme reactions yourself** rather than cutting in movie footage. Acting the Vadivelu reaction yourself is funnier, more personal and 100% original.
4. 🟡 **For any paid or brand Reel**, switch to **Meta Sound Collection** or directly licensed music, or get written clearance from the label. Never use library film songs.
5. 🔵 If a Reel gets muted, swap the audio in-app rather than deleting and reposting.

## 7. English audio: when to use it

| Use English audio when… | Example |
|---|---|
| The joke is **visual and universal** | Rabbit stealing a cable, a gadget failing |
| You're joining a **global trend format** | Trending meme audio with your Tamil-life twist in the text |
| You want to test **broader reach** (as a Trial Reel ✅) | Same concept, Tamil vs English audio, compare sends and reach |

🔵 Target ~15–25% English-audio Reels at first. Let Insights decide the final ratio.

## 8. Audio checklist for Tamil Reels

- [ ] The audio has a clear **role** (hero, villain, tragedy, comedy, nostalgia…)
- [ ] The music *tells* part of the joke or emotion (not just a background bed)
- [ ] The iconic part of the audio starts within the first 1–2 s
- [ ] Beat, drop or switch aligned to the visual payoff, frame-accurate
- [ ] Added from **Instagram's library** (or properly licensed)
- [ ] No movie video footage, only audio plus your footage
- [ ] Not a paid or brand Reel (if it is, use cleared music)
- [ ] Natural sound or SFX layered in for authenticity
- [ ] On-screen text lets non-Tamil speakers follow the visual joke
