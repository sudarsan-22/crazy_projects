## Creative direction (summary)
A 24-second story arc in 10 bars at 100 BPM, with every cut on a beat:
1. **Hook (0–2.4):** heart-eye "LOVE YOU!" close-up in slow-mo + "I built a bear that lives for HEAD PATS"
2. **Intro (2.4–4.8):** the bear's own boot screen "HELLO! I'M MINIMO BEAR", reframed from 4K, plus the name card
3. **Sleep → pat (4.8–7.2):** sleeping "zz" face, "*pat*", wakes with "HELLO SUDARSAN!". The music drops to a lullaby
4. **PAT PAT (7.2–9.6):** close-up pat → "PAT PAT! ^_^". Drums enter
5. **Shelf (9.6–12.0):** cinematic fairy-light shelf, pats and hearts
6. **YUM! HONEY! (12.0–14.4):** a bear eating honey
7. **Montage (14.4–16.8):** four reactions, one per beat, across all three locations
8. **Payoff (16.8–19.2):** slow-mo. **"LOVE YOU!" appears exactly on the 18.0 s accent**, with "he said it first"
9. **Ending (19.2–24.0):** crossfade to a slow push-in on the shelf hearts + end card. Ends on hearts, so it loops back to the hook

Technical: iPhone 4K HDR (HLG) → mobius tone-map → SDR Rec.709. A warm "cozy" grade, with lifted shadows on the dark close-ups so the 3D-print texture shows. Natural audio was near-silent room tone (−42 to −49 LUFS), so it was muted. Light original SFX (pops on expression changes, dings on "HELLO" and "LOVE YOU", soft whooshes on section changes). The re-encoded `44097099…MOV` was skipped because the 4K original (IMG_7191) covers the same moments. The HEIC photos weren't needed.
