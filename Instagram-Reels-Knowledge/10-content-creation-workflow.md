# 10 — Content Creation Workflow (SOP)

> Evidence tags: ✅ Official · 🟡 Reported · 🔵 Practitioner · ⚠️ Theory
> Last researched: 2026-11-07
> This is the standard operating procedure. Follow it for every Reel. Each stage links to the deep-dive document.

---

## 0. Overview

```
1 IDEATE → 2 RESEARCH → 3 SCRIPT → 4 PLAN SHOTS → 5 SHOOT → 6 INGEST
→ 7 ROUGH CUT → 8 FINE CUT → 9 SOUND → 10 COLOUR → 11 TEXT & GRAPHICS
→ 12 QC → 13 EXPORT → 14 PUBLISH → 15 ENGAGE → 16 ANALYSE → (back to 1)
```

🔵 **Time budget for a 30–60 s Reel (solo creator, with practice):**

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Ideate + research + script | 30–60 min |
| Shoot | 30–90 min |
| Edit (cut, sound, colour, text) | 1–3 h |
| QC + export + publish | 15–20 min |
| Engage + analyse | 15–30 min over 3 days |

Batching (section 17) cuts the per-Reel time sharply.

---

## 1. Ideate

- Sources: comments and DMs (real questions), your teardowns ([09 §5](09-instagram-analytics.md#5-analysing-successful-creators-teardown-method)), Instagram search autocomplete, trending audio and formats, the Edits ideas feed (🟡), your own performance log (repackage winners).
- **Idea filter (keep an idea only if YES to all four):**
  1. Does it fit one of my pillars? ([02 §2](02-reels-growth-strategy.md#content-pillars-34))
  2. Would a **stranger** care in 3 seconds?
  3. Can I name **who they'd send it to**?
  4. Can I add something **original** (my take, test, demo or story)?
- Keep an **idea bank** (a note or spreadsheet) with columns: idea · pillar · format · hook draft · priority.

## 2. Research
- Look up the 3 best existing Reels on the topic. How will yours be different or better?
- Gather facts and proof (screenshots, results, sources).
- Pick the keyword people would search ([07 §5](07-instagram-seo-and-optimization.md#5-captions-keywords--hashtags)).

## 3. Script
- Use the template in [05 §8](05-hooks-and-storytelling.md#8-script-template-copy-for-each-reel).
- Write **3–5 hook variants**. Pick the best one (and keep a runner-up for a Trial Reel test).
- Read it aloud with a timer. Cut 20%.

## 4. Plan shots (shot list)
```markdown
| # | Beat | Shot (size/angle) | Movement | A/B-roll | Notes (fps, transition plan) |
|---|------|------------------|----------|----------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | ECU product, low angle | push-in | B | 60fps for ramp, whip-pan out |
| 2 | Context | MCU me to camera | static | A | eye-line upper third |
| … |
```
- Plan transitions **now**: whip pans, covers and matched framing must be filmed ([04 §1](04-professional-editing-techniques.md#1-transitions)).

## 5. Shoot
**Camera settings checklist (🔵):**
- [ ] Lens clean
- [ ] 4K (to allow reframing) or 1080 vertical, at a fixed frame rate (30 / 24, or 60 / 120 for slow-mo)
- [ ] Shutter ≈ 2× fps. AE/AF lock
- [ ] HDR off (or a consistent plan) · log only if you'll grade
- [ ] Vertical orientation (or 4K horizontal for a crop)
- [ ] Mic connected, levels peaking ~−12 dB, headphones on
- [ ] Light: key light at ~45°, face brighter than the background, avoid overhead-only light
- [ ] Frame with the safe zone in mind (eyes upper third, nothing at the edges)

**On set:**
- Record **2–3 takes** of the hook in different energies.
- Record **room tone** (10 s).
- Capture **extra B-roll** for every claim, plus 3–5 generic "texture" shots.
- Slate verbally ("Scene 2 take 3") or clap. It makes syncing and selection faster.

## 6. Ingest & organise

### Folder structure (🔵)
```
Reels/
└── 2026-11-12_reel-015_colour-grading-phone/
    ├── 01_footage/
    │   ├── A-roll/
    │   └── B-roll/
    ├── 02_audio/          (VO, music, SFX used)
    ├── 03_graphics/       (overlays, screenshots, logos)
    ├── 04_project/        (editor project files)
    ├── 05_exports/
    │   ├── reel-015_v1.mp4
    │   └── reel-015_FINAL.mp4
    ├── 06_cover/
    ├── licenses/          (music/SFX licence proofs)
    └── notes.md           (script, caption, hashtags, results)
```
- **Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD_reel-###_short-slug`.
- **Back up** raw footage before formatting cards (3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 media, 1 off-site or cloud).

## 7. Rough cut (story only)
- Make a **selects** stringout of the best takes.
- Assemble the A-roll in story order. Cut every pause and filler word (use AI silence removal to speed this up, [08 §4](08-editing-tools-and-resources.md#4-ai-tools-by-job)).
- Ignore polish. **Does the story work? Is the hook strong?**
- Target length: ≤ script length.

## 8. Fine cut (pacing)
- Add B-roll over every claim. J/L cuts. Punch-ins on emphasis.
- A visual change every 2–5 s ([03 §5](03-video-editing-fundamentals.md#5-pacing-for-retention)).
- Transitions, speed ramps and movement effects ([04](04-professional-editing-techniques.md)).
- Trim the ending and make it loop.
- **Watch muted at 1.5×, then with sound at 1×.** Cut anywhere you get bored.
- **Picture lock.**

## 9. Sound
- Clean dialogue (noise reduction → EQ → compression).
- Music placed, beat-synced and ducked.
- SFX on transitions, text pops and impacts.
- Mix to about −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP ([06 §6](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#6-mixing-levels-for-reels)).

## 10. Colour
- Correct (exposure, WB) → match shots → apply the brand look → protect skin.
- Check on a phone at medium brightness ([04 §5](04-professional-editing-techniques.md#5-colour-correction-and-grading)).

## 11. Text & graphics
- Hook title (0–2 s), captions, labels, arrows, progress cues.
- Safe-zone overlay ON while placing, OFF for export.
- Spell-check every caption.

## 12. QC (quality control) — watch the full Reel on a phone
- [ ] Frame 1 is strong. The hook lands in under 3 s
- [ ] No typos. Captions synced
- [ ] Nothing important under the UI (top, bottom or right)
- [ ] Audio clear on a phone speaker. No clipping, no clicks
- [ ] Colour consistent. Skin natural
- [ ] No black frames, flash frames or stray clips
- [ ] No watermarks or third-party logos you don't own
- [ ] ≤3 min. Ending loops or lands
- [ ] Music licensed for this use
- [ ] Would **I** send this to someone?

## 13. Export
- H.264 MP4 · 1080×1920 · native fps · ~10–20 Mbps VBR · AAC 48 kHz 320 kbps ([07 §1](07-instagram-seo-and-optimization.md#1-video-specs-cheat-sheet)).
- Save as `reel-###_FINAL.mp4`. Transfer to the phone via cable, AirDrop or cloud, keeping the **original quality**. Avoid messaging apps that compress.

## 14. Publish
- [ ] "Upload at highest quality" is ON
- [ ] Trial Reel or normal? ([02 §5](02-reels-growth-strategy.md#5-testing-system-trial-reels--iteration))
- [ ] Custom cover (3:4-safe)
- [ ] Caption: keyword hook line → value → CTA → 3–5 hashtags
- [ ] Tags: collaborators, location, products
- [ ] Add in-app music if using library audio. Set the volume balance
- [ ] Auto-captions ON
- [ ] AI label if it contains realistic AI-generated media ✅
- [ ] Paid partnership label if sponsored ✅
- [ ] Post or schedule 30–60 min before the audience peak

## 15. Engage (first hours and days)
- Reply to comments thoughtfully. Pin the best comment.
- Share the Reel to your Story (with a sticker or question) for follower reach.
- Save good comments as ideas for "reply" Reels.
- 🔵 Don't delete and repost in the first hours. Distribution often takes 24–72 h to build.

## 16. Analyse
- At **72 h** and **day 7**: log the metrics in the tracking sheet ([09 §6](09-instagram-analytics.md#6-your-tracking-sheet-start-in-phase-5)).
- Run the diagnosis tree ([09 §4](09-instagram-analytics.md#4-diagnosis-tree)).
- Write **one lesson** in `notes.md` and the performance log.
- Feed the lessons into the next ideation round.

---

## 17. Batching system (🔵 sustainable 3–5 Reels/week)

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| **Day 1 (2 h)** | Ideate and script 4–6 Reels |
| **Day 2 (2–3 h)** | Shoot all of them (same lighting and setup, outfit changes) |
| **Day 3–4 (4–6 h)** | Edit (rough cuts for all, then fine cuts) |
| **Day 5 (1 h)** | QC, export, schedule the whole week. Write captions |
| **Daily (15–30 min)** | Engage, Stories, collect ideas, log analytics |

🔵 **Templates speed everything up.** Phase 3 will produce an editor project template (tracks, caption style, safe zone, SFX bin, colour preset) so each Reel starts 80% configured.
