## 1. How Tamil BGM-led edits work

Tamil cinema audio arrives with **built-in meaning**: an audience hears a mass BGM and expects a hero. Editing to it means using **those expectations** in one of three ways:

| Mode | What you do | Example from your life |
|---|---|---|
| **Fulfil** | Give the audio exactly the image it promises, done well | A real cinematic R15 golden-hour shot on a mass BGM |
| **Inflate** (contrast) | Give a *tiny* moment the full cinematic treatment | The rabbit walking to its food bowl, with a hero entry |
| **Break** (switch) | Fulfil the promise, then snap to reality | Mass BGM build → R15 doesn't start → record scratch → comedy track |

🔵 **Inflate** and **Break** are your signature ("Bruh Cinema"). **Fulfil** belongs in the "Feel" 20% (Road Diaries, pet bonding).

## 2. Anatomy of a Tamil BGM (find these points before cutting)

Most mass and cinematic BGMs follow a recognisable shape. Mark these on the timeline first ([24 §3](24-beat-sync-and-sound-design.md#3-marking-the-audio-before-cutting)):

```
[INTRO/RISE]──►[HOOK PHRASE / "THEME"]──►[BUILD]──►[DROP / HIT]──►[RIDE]──►[TAIL]
   tension        the recognisable bit      riser      the big moment   groove     fade
```

| Point | What to put there |
|---|---|
| Intro / rise | Establishing or teaser shots. Slow push-in. Silhouette |
| **Hook phrase** | **Frame 1 of your Reel should usually start here.** Viewers recognise the BGM instantly ([15 §5](15-tamil-music-and-audio-strategy.md#5-matching-music-with-edits-technical)) |
| Build / riser | Speed ramp *into* the hero moment. Tightening cuts |
| **Drop / hit** | **The hero frame**: freeze + name card, reveal, impact, or **the BGM switch** |
| Ride | Cut on downbeats. Supporting shots |
| Tail | Loop point or ending punchline |

**Trim rule:** Instagram lets you choose the **segment** of a library song. Choose the segment so the **hook phrase starts at 0.0 s** and the **drop lands on your hero moment** ([28 §3](28-reels-export-and-publishing.md#3-adding-instagram-library-audio-in-app)).

## 3. Recipe cards by audio role

Each recipe: audio points → picture → effects → SFX → text. Timings are starting points. Adapt them to the footage.

### 🦸 R1 · Mass hero entry (Inflate)
| Audio point | Picture | Effect | SFX | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook phrase (0–1.5 s) | Low-angle shot of feet, wheels or paws. Slow push-in | 120/60 fps slow-mo · MovieMode grade | — | — |
| Build (1.5–3 s) | 2–3 quick detail cuts (helmet strap, key, eyes) on beats | Cuts on downbeats | Whoosh into each | — |
| **Drop (≈3 s)** | **Freeze frame** on the subject's "pose" | Zoom 100→108% over 6 frames · darken bg 30% | **Impact hit** on the freeze frame | **Name card** slides in (S1): "R15 — MASS HERO" |
| Ride | Hero walks or drives on | Back to 100% speed | — | — |
| *(Break version)* | → reality moment | **BGM switch** (§4) | Record scratch | "BRUH" stamp (S3) |

### 😈 R2 · Villain theme (Inflate) — great for the pets
- Slow **push-in** on the "villain" (the rabbit staring, a bitten cable), centred, eye level
- **Cool or dark grade** (teal shadows, −exposure), slight vignette
- Cut to "evidence" shots on each heavy BGM hit (chewed wire, empty bowl)
- End on a **close-up stare** held for 1–1.5 s (the comedy is in the hold) → villain name card
- SFX: low boom on the reveal. Optional thunder for absurdity

### 😭 R3 · Tragedy / sad violin (Break or Inflate)
- After the fail: **slow motion** (50%) of the aftermath, **desaturate** (−40% saturation), slight blue tint
- Slow push-in on your face or the broken object
- Text in a sad-movie style: small, centred, fade-in: *"He tried his best."*
- Hold the last frame 1.5 s. Tragedy needs time to land

### 🤣 R4 · Comedy dialogue (lip-sync / dub)
See §5 for the precise technique. Picture rules:
- **Medium close-up** of the "speaker" (you, or the pet mapped to the voice)
- **Reaction shot** of the "listener" on the punchline
- **Zoom punch** (100→115%, 4 frames) on the key word
- **Translation subtitle** for non-Tamil viewers (§6)

### 💕 R5 · Romantic melody (Inflate) — you × your R15, you × a gadget
- Soft focus or bloom (light glow), warm grade, slow motion, **eye-line match** (you look → cut to the bike "looking back")
- Gentle cross-dissolves (8–12 frames). The only place in your style where dissolves belong
- Payoff: break the romance (the bike's fuel light, the gadget's price tag) → switch

### 🕵️ R6 · Suspense / thriller (Inflate) — "Who ate the cable?"
- Fast **cuts on hits** with Dutch angles and extreme close-ups of clues
- **Text timestamps** for faux drama: "11:42 PM"
- Build with a riser → **reveal on the drop** (the rabbit, mid-chew)
- SFX: heartbeat or ticking under the build, a hit on the reveal

### 🌅 R7 · Nostalgic melody (Fulfil) — Road Diaries
- Long smooth shots (1.5–3 s), **cut on the phrase**, not on every beat
- Warm film grade + light grain ([25 §5](25-cinematic-and-comedy-editing.md#5-colour-correction--cinematic-grading))
- One line of relatable text (Tanglish) mid-way. Ending loops to the opening shot
- No SFX except natural sound (engine hum, wind) underneath at low level

### 🏋️ R8 · Training-montage BGM — DIY builds
- **Timelapse / speed-up** (4–16×) segments, cut on every beat
- Step numbers as text (S4-style tag)
- **Reveal the finished build on the drop**, then the comedy beat (rabbit sits on it)

### 💃 R9 · Kuthu / energetic song
- Cut **on every beat** or every second beat. Match on-screen motion to the rhythm
- Use **flash cuts** (2–3 frame white flash) on accents. Sparingly
- Great for gadget unboxings, room glow-ups and celebration moments

## 4. The BGM switch, step by step

Your core comedy mechanic ([16 S2](16-editing-identity-and-reel-formats.md#1-the-editing-identity-bruh-cinema)).

```
...BGM A (epic) builds ──► [last 0.3–0.5 s: the fail begins on screen]
                         ──► SWITCH FRAME: the exact frame the "bruh" becomes visible
                               • cut BGM A dead (no fade) or record-scratch
                               • 0.2–0.5 s of silence or natural sound only
                               • zoom punch 100→112% (3–4 frames) + optional 2-frame shake
                               • grade snaps MovieMode → Reality (S5)
                         ──► BGM B (comedy / sad) starts on a clear downbeat
```

| Detail | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Switch frame | The first frame where the fail is **unambiguous** | Too early spoils it, too late loses momentum |
| BGM A | Let it reach its **build** or **just before its drop**, then cut. Denying the drop *is* the joke | Expectation peaks right before the drop |
| Silence gap | 0.2–0.5 s ([06 §4](06-audio-and-sound-design.md#4-sound-design-layering-sfx)) | Gives the brain time to register the fail |
| BGM B entry | On its own strongest recognisable phrase | Instant genre recognition |
| In-app audio | Instagram's Reels editor supports **multitrack audio: up to 20 library tracks per Reel**, placed next to each other or overlapping, aligned with clips and text (launched globally July 2024) | 🟡 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/16/instagram-now-allows-users-to-add-multiple-audio-tracks-to-reels), [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/instagram-users-can-now-add-up-to-20-tracks-to-a-single-reel1/)) |

### Building the switch with licensed audio (best first)
1. ✅ **Two library tracks in the Instagram Reels editor (multitrack):** add BGM A (the epic one) and trim it to end at the switch frame. Then "add to the mix" BGM B (comedy or sad), starting right after the 0.2–0.5 s gap. Both stay licensed. 🟡 The multitrack mix becomes an **audio mix attributed to you** that others can reuse. Lyrics can be shown for only one track ([Label Worx](https://support.label-worx.com/hc/en-us/articles/20507455423378-Instagram-Create-Engaging-Reels-with-Multiple-Audio-Tracks)). ⚠️ The multitrack mix may not link to each song's audio page the way a single-track Reel does, so check how it appears after posting
2. **Edits app:** reports describe a multi-track audio timeline connected to Instagram's music library 🟡 ([NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-edits/)). Check the current behaviour in your app
3. **One audio that already contains the switch:** many Tamil comedy audios on Instagram are already "BGM → scratch → dialogue" edits. Simplest if one fits. Verify the source with screenshots ([22](22-instagram-audio-discovery.md))
4. **Library track A + your own sound for B:** A in-app, and the reality part carried by your voice, natural sound, or a royalty-free sting baked into the export
5. ❌ **Avoid:** baking copyrighted film audio into the exported file. It loses licensing and audio-page linking, and risks muting or blocking ✅

**My production default:** I render the video **without film music** (natural sound + SFX + royalty-free stings only), and give you exact in-app timings for tracks A and B ([28 §3](28-reels-export-and-publishing.md#3-adding-instagram-library-audio-in-app)). Every plan states which option it uses.

## 5. Dialogue lip-sync & dub technique

| Step | Technique |
|---|---|
| 1. Get the exact audio | Use the Instagram library version (the same clip you'll post with). Play it while filming so your performance matches its timing |
| 2. Film | Play the audio on a speaker while recording. Perform big. Mouth shapes matter more than accuracy of words. Do 3+ takes |
| 3. Align | Line up the **first hard consonant** (plosives: ப/b, ட/d, க/k sounds) with the frame where your lips open. Then slip ±1–2 frames until it reads right |
| 4. Check | Watch at 0.5× speed, then at 1× on a phone. Lip-sync errors over ~2 frames are visible 🔵 |
| 5. Punch | Zoom punch or cut to a reaction on the **final word** of the line |
| Pets / objects "speaking" | Cut to the pet at the **start** of each line, and hold on its face (no lip movement needed). Comedy comes from a **reaction** (ear twitch, head turn) landing on the punch word |
| You *acting* the classic reaction | Recreate famous comic reactions yourself instead of cutting in movie footage. It's original and fully yours ✅ ([15 §6](15-tamil-music-and-audio-strategy.md#6-licensing--legal-india-specific)) |

## 6. Subtitles for Tamil audio

| Situation | Subtitle approach |
|---|---|
| Iconic dialogue (Tamil audience knows it) | **Tamil script or Tanglish** quote in big punch text, plus a **small English translation** below for everyone else |
| Song lyrics carrying the joke | Only the punch lyric line, with a translation |
| Your own speech in Tamil | Tanglish captions (how you talk) + optional English |
| Pure BGM (no words) | No subtitles. Use **situation text** ("POV: …", name cards) |

Rendering: Tamil script uses **Noto Sans Tamil** 🧪 ([21 §6](21-professional-reels-editing.md#6-text--subtitle-production-standard)). Proofread Tamil spelling with you before rendering, because I may make spelling or transliteration mistakes in Tamil.

## 7. Cultural & safety notes for Tamil audio content (🔵)

- **Fan wars:** Hero-fan rivalries are intense. Avoid content that mocks one star's fans to praise another's. Celebrate, don't provoke
- **Politics, caste, religion:** Many comedy clips carry political or community undertones. Skip any audio whose joke depends on mocking a community, caste or religion
- **Respect the artists:** Vikkals' successful approach was affectionate, "to not hurt or belittle" ([TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/meet-instagrammers-who-recreate-tamil-song-recordings-hilarious-twist-166121)). Parody the *situation*, not the artist
- **Devotional music:** Use with care. Using it for comedy can offend

## 8. Tamil BGM edit checklist

- [ ] Audio mode chosen: Fulfil / Inflate / Break
- [ ] Hook phrase starts at 0.0 s (segment chosen accordingly)
- [ ] Drop or hit aligned to the hero frame (±1 frame)
- [ ] Switch built per §4, with the two-track solution stated
- [ ] Lip-sync within ~2 frames (if dialogue)
- [ ] Translation subtitle for key dialogue
- [ ] No movie footage. Only audio plus your footage
- [ ] Culturally safe (§7)
