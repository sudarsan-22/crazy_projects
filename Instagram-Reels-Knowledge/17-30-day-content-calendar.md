## Posting rhythm

| Slot | Default day* | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reel A | **Monday** | Comedy (P1) or series opener |
| Reel B | **Wednesday** | Pets (P2) or Builds (P3) |
| Reel C | **Saturday** | Roads (P4), Builds (P3), or a "double-down" repeat of the best format |
| Trial Reel | Thursday (weeks 2 and 3) | Hook or audio experiment, non-followers only ✅ |
| Stories | 3–5 days/week | Behind the scenes, polls ("which pet episode next?"), sharing your new Reel |

\* Move the days and times to match **Insights → Audience → Most active times** once you have it ([07 §7](07-instagram-seo-and-optimization.md#7-posting-timing--frequency)). Post 30–60 min before the peak.

## Weekly production routine (batching)

Based on [10 §17](10-content-creation-workflow.md#17-batching-system--sustainable-35-reelsweek), adapted for 3 Reels a week:

| When | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Sunday** | Review last week's numbers (15 min) → script next week's 3 Reels plus the trial → shot lists | 1.5 h |
| **Sunday / Monday** | Shoot skits (P1) and builds (P3) in one session. Same lighting, outfit changes | 1.5–2 h |
| **Opportunistic** | Pet footage (calm moments, whenever they happen). Road shots at weekend golden hour | — |
| **Mon–Fri evenings** | Edit about 1 Reel per evening (1–2 h each) | 3–6 h total |
| **Before each post** | QC checklist ([11 §5](11-best-practices-and-mistakes.md#5-one-page-pre-publish-checklist)) plus identity check ([16 §8](16-editing-identity-and-reel-formats.md#8-identity-consistency-check-before-publishing)) | 15 min |
| **Daily** | Reply to comments, 1–2 Stories, note ideas from comments | 15–20 min |

**Total ≈ 8–11 h/week.** If that's too much, drop to **2 main Reels a week** and keep the trial Reels. Consistency beats volume ([02 §8](02-reels-growth-strategy.md#8-posting-cadence-and-consistency)).

### Day 30 review questions
1. Which **pillar** had the best sends/reach and follows/reach? Should the weights shift?
2. Which **format** (F1–F11) had the lowest skip rate and the highest avg % watched?
3. **Tamil vs English audio:** any difference in reach or sends?
4. Which **signatures** appear in the top 3 Reels? Keep them and drop the ones that don't help.
5. Did the hook test (T1) show a winning hook type?
6. Did any Reel earn "how?" comments? That's demand for F7.
7. Was the workload sustainable? Adjust the cadence honestly.
→ Write the answers into [13](13-my-instagram-account-analysis.md) and update [14](14-creator-identity-and-content-pillars.md) weights, then build the next 30-day calendar.

### 30-day success criteria (process goals, no invented follower targets)
- [ ] 12–13 main Reels plus 2 Trial Reels published, all logged
- [ ] Baseline medians established for every key ratio
- [ ] At least 1 hook test and 1 audio-language test completed
- [ ] Best pillar and best format identified with data
- [ ] Brand kit used consistently (≥3 signatures per Reel)
- [ ] Zero originality or eligibility issues in Account Status
