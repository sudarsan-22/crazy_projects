## 1. The 20 best practices

### Strategy
1. ✅ **Make it for non-followers.** Reels and Explore are mainly discovery surfaces. → [01](01-instagram-algorithm.md)
2. 🔵 **One niche, 3–4 pillars, one promise.** → [02](02-reels-growth-strategy.md)
3. 🟡 **Design every Reel for a send.** Name the person a viewer would DM it to. → [01](01-instagram-algorithm.md)
4. ✅ **Be original.** Film it yourself, or transform it substantially. → [01 §5](01-instagram-algorithm.md#5-originality-rules-2024--2026)
5. ✅ **Test with Trial Reels.** → [02 §5](02-reels-growth-strategy.md#5-testing-system-trial-reels--iteration)

### Content
6. ✅ **Hook in under 3 seconds**, using visual, text and spoken hooks together. → [05](05-hooks-and-storytelling.md)
7. 🔵 **No intros, no logos, no fade-ins.** Frame 1 is content.
8. 🔵 **Put the payoff in the final 10–20%.** End on a loop or a clean landing.
9. 🔵 **Be as long as the story needs**, and ✅ ≤3 min for recommendations.
10. 🔵 **One CTA**, placed after the value.

### Editing
11. 🔵 **A visual change every 2–5 s.** Cut every pause. → [03](03-video-editing-fundamentals.md)
12. 🔵 **Show, don't tell.** Every claim gets B-roll or a graphic.
13. 🔵 **Use motivated transitions with SFX**, under 0.5 s. → [04](04-professional-editing-techniques.md)
14. 🔵 **Prioritise audio:** clean dialogue, ducked music, about −14 LUFS. → [06](06-audio-and-sound-design.md)
15. 🔵 **Correct, then grade. Protect skin tones.** Check on a phone.
16. 🔵 **Captions inside the safe zone**, 2 lines max, high contrast, and proofread.

### Publishing & analytics
17. ✅ **Export 1080×1920 H.264** and turn on "Upload at highest quality". → [07](07-instagram-seo-and-optimization.md)
18. ✅ **Keywords in the name field, bio and caption line 1. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags.**
19. ✅ **Check Account Status** whenever reach drops.
20. ✅/🔵 **Judge by ratios** (skip rate, % watched, sends, saves and follows per reach) against your own median. → [09](09-instagram-analytics.md)

## 2. The 30 most common mistakes

### Algorithm & eligibility
| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Instead |
|---|---|
| Uploading with a TikTok or CapCut watermark | Export clean files ✅ |
| Reposting others' Reels or memes with only a credit | Remix, add commentary, or share to your Story ✅ |
| Reels over 3 min when you want reach | ≤3 min. Save long-form for followers ✅ |
| Horizontal video with black bars | Full-frame 9:16 ✅ |
| Muted Reels or uncleared music (gets muted) | Always include licensed audio ✅ |
| Ignoring Account Status | Check it monthly and whenever reach drops ✅ |
| Blaming a "shadowban" | Run the diagnosis tree in [09 §4](09-instagram-analytics.md#4-diagnosis-tree) |

### Hooks & story
| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| "Hey guys, welcome back…" | Start mid-action with the promise |
| Hook with no context for strangers ("Part 2 of what I said…") | A stranger-proof hook. Recap in one line |
| Clickbait that doesn't pay off | Deliver the promise. Instagram surveys viewers on "worth your time" ✅ |
| Payoff in the first 5 seconds, then nothing | Open the loop early, pay it off late |
| Long outro or "follow for more" ramble | End fast and loop |
| Multiple CTAs | One CTA |

### Editing
| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| Leaving pauses, breaths and "ums" | Jump-cut them all out |
| One static shot for 20+ seconds | Punch-ins, B-roll, graphics |
| Preset spin or page-curl transitions everywhere | Motivated cuts and match cuts |
| Text under the like/comment buttons or bottom caption | Use a safe-zone overlay |
| Typos in captions | Proofread every caption |
| Too much text covering the screen | Keep text concise ✅ (text-heavy frames are a negative) |
| Music louder than the voice | Duck 18–25 dB under speech |
| The same whoosh 30 times | Vary SFX. Use them only for emphasis |
| Heavy LUT at 100%, orange skin | Correct first, then the LUT at reduced intensity |
| Mixing HDR and SDR clips | Pick one pipeline (usually SDR) |
| Slow-mo from 30 fps footage (stutter) | Shoot 60/120 fps, or use optical flow carefully |
| Grading on a laptop only | Check on a phone at medium brightness |

### Publishing & growth
| ❌ | ✅ |
|---|---|
| 30 hashtags or #viral #fyp | 3–5 relevant ones (5 is the cap 🟡) |
| Keywords only in hashtags or comments | Keywords in caption line 1 ✅ |
| No custom cover, random grid | A 3:4-safe cover template ✅ |
| Posting whenever, with no consistency | A cadence you can sustain (3–5 a week) |
| Judging a Reel by likes or views alone | Ratios vs your median |
| Deleting a Reel after 2 hours because it's "flopping" | Wait 72 h. Re-edit and test instead |
| Chasing every trend outside your niche | Fit trends to your pillars |

## 3. Banned / harmful growth tactics — never use

✅ These violate Instagram's Terms of Use or Community Guidelines, or damage your signals:

| Tactic | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| **Buying followers, likes, views or comments** | Inauthentic behaviour violates the guidelines. Fake accounts never watch, so your ratios collapse and targeting breaks. Risk of restriction |
| **Bots / automation for follows, likes or comments** | Against the Terms of Use. Action blocks and account restriction |
| **Follow/unfollow schemes** | Spammy. Low-quality followers. Possible restrictions |
| **Engagement pods / comment groups** | Inauthentic engagement from non-target audiences confuses recommendations |
| **"Giveaway for follows" with fake entrants** | Attracts freebie-seekers who never engage again |
| **Reposting viral content from others** | Ineligible for recommendations (April 2026 policy) ✅ |
| **Misleading clickbait or engagement bait** | Lowers "worth your time" scores. Mosseri warns against forced bait |
| **Using copyrighted music or footage without rights** | Muting, blocks, takedowns, strikes |

## 4. Disclosures & ethics

- ✅ **Paid partnerships:** Use the **Paid partnership** label for any sponsored content (Instagram's Branded Content Policy; legal requirements such as the FTC in the US, ASCI in India and the CMA/ASA in the UK).
- ✅ **AI content:** Add the **"AI info"** label for realistic AI-generated or AI-altered media. 🟡 AI-persona accounts must use the **"AI-generated profile"** label (from 31 Aug 2026).
- 🔵 **Affiliate links / product tags:** Disclose clearly.
- 🔵 **Consent:** Get permission before featuring identifiable people, especially in private settings. Never clone voices or faces without consent.
- 🔵 **Accuracy:** Fact-check tips and claims. Mark opinions as opinions.

## 5. One-page pre-publish checklist

```
STRATEGY  [ ] fits a pillar  [ ] stranger-proof  [ ] "send-to" person named  [ ] original
HOOK      [ ] frame 1 strong  [ ] text hook ≤8 words  [ ] spoken hook ≤0.5 s
STORY     [ ] open loop  [ ] payoff in last 10–20%  [ ] loop/clean end  [ ] one CTA
EDIT      [ ] no pauses  [ ] change every 2–5 s  [ ] B-roll for claims  [ ] safe zone
AUDIO     [ ] licensed  [ ] voice clear on phone speaker  [ ] ~−14 LUFS, ≤−1 dBTP
COLOUR    [ ] consistent  [ ] skin natural  [ ] checked on phone
TEXT      [ ] proofread  [ ] ≤2 lines  [ ] readable in time
EXPORT    [ ] 1080×1920 H.264 MP4  [ ] native fps  [ ] no watermark  [ ] ≤3 min
PUBLISH   [ ] HQ upload ON  [ ] 3:4-safe cover  [ ] keyword caption line 1
          [ ] 3–5 hashtags  [ ] tags/location  [ ] auto-captions  [ ] labels (AI/paid)
          [ ] Trial Reel decision  [ ] timed before audience peak
AFTER     [ ] reply to comments  [ ] share to Story  [ ] log at 72 h and day 7
```
